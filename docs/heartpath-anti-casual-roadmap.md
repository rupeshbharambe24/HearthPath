# HeartPath Anti-Casual Product Roadmap

## Summary
HeartPath is a trust-first relationship system, not a mainstream dating marketplace. The product should optimize for fewer but better matches, verified identity, slower mutual progression, and real relationship maintenance. The differentiator is protecting users from casual, fake, addictive behavior and guiding them toward one meaningful bond.

## Core Promise
- verified students only
- limited intentional discovery
- friendship and trust first
- intimacy by mutual permission
- exclusivity when serious
- relationship tools after matching, not just chat

## Product Rules
- Discovery is curated daily, not endless.
- Monetization follows trust and retention, not addiction loops.
- Mutuals and campus circles are optional trust enhancers, not the primary matching engine.

## Key Changes

### 1. Intentional discovery over marketplace mechanics
- de-emphasize broad browse-first exploration
- move to a limited daily batch of high-quality suggestions
- restrict outbound invitations each day
- rank suggestions by intent alignment, pace compatibility, communication fit, value overlap, boundary compatibility, and lifestyle fit
- support discovery modes:
  - `friendship_first`
  - `slow_burn`
  - `serious_only`
  - `same_campus`
- show plain-language "why this match was chosen" explanations
- let users dismiss "not a fit for now" without gamifying rejection

### 2. Make trust and seriousness visible
- keep strict verified college access
- show visible trust badges:
  - `Email Verified`
  - `College Verified`
- later support:
  - `Photo Verified`
  - `Identity Verified`
- show profile depth and seriousness indicators:
  - intent declared
  - pace declared
  - boundaries declared
  - norms accepted
- include HeartPath norms during onboarding:
  - no fake identity
  - no intimacy pressure
  - no hookup-first behavior
  - respect for pacing

### 3. Make stage progression the core loop
- every accepted connection begins at low-access trust building
- each stage requires one proposal and one explicit response
- no stage skipping
- decline and deferral trigger cooldowns
- stage unlocks should include:
  - deeper profile details
  - richer prompts
  - voice notes
  - private gallery
  - shared memory vault
  - shared AI recap
  - exclusivity mode

### 4. Build the real relationship space
- make the shared relationship space the center after acceptance
- include:
  - shared dashboard
  - weekly check-ins
  - gratitude notes
  - memory vault
  - stage history
  - boundaries and agreements summary
  - milestones and anniversaries
  - exclusivity home
- add repair and closure flows:
  - pause mode
  - repair prompts
  - respectful archive after breakup
  - cooldown before rediscovery

### 5. Use AI as support
- use AI for:
  - profile writing help
  - match explanations
  - private reflection prompts
  - shared memory organization
  - recap of milestones
  - searchable relationship archive
  - gratitude suggestions
  - neutral long-chat summaries
- do not use AI for:
  - flirting on behalf of users
  - fake chemistry
  - personality diagnosis
  - toxicity labels
  - pushing intimacy progression

### 6. Add healthy signals and safety
- build explainable user-facing signals instead of a public red-flag meter:
  - `Trust`
  - `Mutuality`
  - `Respect`
  - `Consistency`
  - `Comfort`
  - `Openness`
- derive them from explicit in-app behavior
- keep stronger risk scoring private for moderation only
- add:
  - report
  - block
  - restrict
  - anti-pressure escalation
  - repeated boundary-violation logging
  - moderation review

### 7. Grow through campus trust density
- launch campus by campus
- support ambassador and invite programs
- use waitlists by college domain
- optionally show:
  - same branch
  - same campus zone
  - mutual circle available
- allow shareable milestone artifacts only after a relationship exists

### 8. Monetize around seriousness
- delay aggressive monetization until trust and retention are healthy
- good paid layers:
  - `HeartPath Plus`
  - `HeartPath Verified+`
  - `HeartPath Couple`
- avoid:
  - boosts
  - unlimited likes
  - attention gambling
  - manipulative scarcity loops

## Delivery Phases

### Phase 2: Intentional discovery and compatibility
- richer profile inputs for values, pace, intent, lifestyle, and boundaries
- curated daily suggestions instead of open browsing
- compatibility explanations and discovery modes
- invitation caps and daily suggestion logic

### Phase 3: Stage-driven progression and anti-pressure
- stronger mutual stage progression
- stage rituals and cooldowns
- permission-linked unlocks
- reduced early intimacy pressure

### Phase 4: Shared relationship space
- deeper weekly check-ins and gratitude notes
- expanded memory vault and milestone resurfacing
- exclusive couple-mode home
- respectful pause/archive flows

### Phase 5: AI support and healthy signals
- AI profile assistant and match explainer
- recap tools and archive search
- explainable trust, mutuality, and respect signals
- strong shared-AI consent rules

### Phase 6: Campus growth and careful monetization
- ambassador, waitlist, and campus launch mechanics
- optional mutual-circle trust features
- premium couple and verification features
- retention and relationship-success analytics

## Acceptance Focus
- discovery stays limited and intentional
- compatibility explanations stay coherent and user-facing
- stage progression never skips and never bypasses consent
- exclusive users leave dating-mode discovery
- AI remains assistive and opt-in
- premium features improve quality without becoming pay-to-win
